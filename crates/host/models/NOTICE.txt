The neural text recogniser's model

ppocr-rec.onnx is PaddleOCR's en_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec, the English text recognition model of the
PP-OCRv5 series, and ppocr-dict.txt is its dictionary, ppocrv5_en_dict.txt: the characters it can
read, one a line. PaddleOCR is the PaddlePaddle project's, published under the Apache License,
Version 2.0, whose text is LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt beside this file. The model came out with
PaddleOCR 3.2.0 (2025-08-21), in PaddlePaddle's own format:

    https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
    https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/en_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec

The file here is not in that format. It is the conversion to ONNX that monkt/paddleocr-onnx
publishes on Hugging Face, also under the Apache License 2.0, as languages/english/rec.onnx, with
the dictionary beside it as languages/english/dict.txt, both as its commit e354f8b of 2025-10-07
left them:

    https://huggingface.co/monkt/paddleocr-onnx

The two files here are those byte for byte, by the digests Hugging Face gives for them, and the
dictionary is also PaddleOCR's ppocr/utils/dict/ppocrv5_en_dict.txt byte for byte:

    ppocr-rec.onnx  7830888 bytes  SHA-256 4e16deb22c4da6468bdca539b2cd3c8687825538b67109177c47d359ab994cd7
    ppocr-dict.txt     1416 bytes  SHA-256 e025a66d31f327ba0c232e03f407ae8d105e1e709e7ccb3f408aa778c24e70d6

The conversion was made with paddle2onnx (https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/Paddle2ONNX), as the
graph says: "PaddlePaddle Graph in PIR mode", the program format of PaddlePaddle 3, values named
p2o.*, ONNX opset 14 and an input 48 pixels high. (The config.json beside it there says opset 11
and 32; the graph is what the application feeds.) Which revision of PaddleOCR's model it was
converted from is not said there. The two files came into this repository on 2026-06-21, without
their source; it was found on 2026-10-24, by the digests.

Automation Platform reads small regions of the screen with it: on Windows it is inside the
executable, and a macOS package carries it as AutomationPlatform.app/Contents/Resources/
ppocr-rec.onnx. Both packages carry this notice and the licence in their licences folder, as
paddleocr-model-NOTICE.txt and paddleocr-model-Apache-2.0.txt.
